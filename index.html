ScriptLilt Support

Need help? Email noahjiang2@gmail.com.

ScriptLilt helps you write and import scripts, rehearse, record video, manage takes, and optionally show a prompt on a supported outward-facing display. The app remains usable without that display.

When contacting support, include your app version, device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send private scripts or recordings unless necessary. We will not ask for your Apple Account password or payment card details.

Common questions

Can I rehearse without camera access?

Yes. Choose rehearsal from your script library.

Why is the camera unavailable?

Check camera and microphone access in iOS Settings → ScriptLilt. Recording requires a compatible physical device; a simulator cannot validate real camera capture.

Why is the outward display unavailable?

It requires supported hardware, an appropriate device posture, and iOS 27.1 or later. It is not needed for normal prompting or recording.

How does Pro work?

Free use includes up to three saved takes at once. Delete a take to free a slot. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes this limit. If you already purchased Pro, use Restore Purchases in the app with the same Apple Account.

Where are my scripts and takes?

They are stored in the app's local documents. You can share a script or take, and save a take to Photos. Deleting an item in the app does not delete copies exported elsewhere.
